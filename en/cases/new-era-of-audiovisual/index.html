CASE 03 · BRAND & CAMPAIGN

The New Era of Audiovisual

Visual identity and advertising campaign for the launch of an online course on filming, video editing and scriptwriting.

ROLE

Visual identity and campaign

CLIENT

João Gundin

YEAR

2026

TOOLS

Figma

01 — CONTEXT

ONLINE COURSE · LIVE LAUNCH

An audiovisual course that needed a brand and a campaign worthy of its launch.

The client

João Gundin works in audiovisual and teaches filming, video editing and scriptwriting in an online course. The launch opened with a 100% live masterclass, "The New Era of Audiovisual", on May 27 at 7 pm.

The brief

João asked for a visual identity for the course and an advertising campaign to promote it, with ads and social media pieces that would lead people to sign up.

ASK

Visual identity and advertising campaign

AUDIENCE

People who want to make a living from filming and video editing

GOAL

Generate sign-ups for the course

02 — CHALLENGE

Give the course its own brand and turn attention into sign-ups.

Create an identity strong enough to stand out in the feed.

Communicate the masterclass date, time and live format without noise.

Present the offer clearly: guarantee, bonuses and last tickets.

Adapt the same campaign to horizontal and vertical formats.

03 — CONCEPT

5 STEPS

Red, black and cinema light: the first take on the new era.

08 — LEARNINGS

Takeaway

Identity and campaign born together. Creating the concept pieces before finalizing the brand made it possible to test the idea in real use, and the campaign inherited an already validated language. The result shows the weight of consistent communication when launching a digital product.
