01 — CONTEXT

FEED · STORIES · CAROUSEL

Social media creatives: messages that need to land in seconds.

The work

Feed, stories and carousel pieces created for 17 brands from very different sectors: clinics and doctors, industry, retail, services and fitness. Many were designed as ads, with a strong hook, a clear offer and a direct call to action.

How I approach the pieces

Each brand has its own language, but the logic repeats: one idea per piece, clear hierarchy and contrast to stop the scroll mid-feed. Vertical formats and carousels fill the whole phone screen and tell the message in steps.
