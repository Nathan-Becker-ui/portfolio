CASE 02 · LANDING PAGE

Car House Powerchip

Landing page for a Londrina workshop specialized in engine and transmission remapping for premium cars, with a call for a quote in every section.

ROLE

Page design (UX/UI), at the agency

CLIENT

Car House Powerchip

YEAR

2025

TOOLS

Figma

01 — CONTEXT

LONDRINA, BRAZIL

A performance workshop that wanted to be seen as the go-to name in Londrina.

The client

Car House is a workshop focused on premium cars, specialized in engine (ECU) and transmission (TCU) remapping. With expert service and its own dynamometer, it needed a digital presence that matched the work done in the shop.

The brief

Car House asked for a landing page that would reposition the brand as the reference for remapping and performance in Londrina and turn visits into quote requests. The page had to present the ECU and TCU services, the workshop's strengths, delivered projects and customer reviews, using the visual material the shop already had.

ASK

Conversion landing page

AUDIENCE

Owners of premium and imported cars in Londrina and the region

GOAL

Reposition the brand and generate more quote requests

02 — CHALLENGE

Reposition the brand and turn visits into quote requests.

Reposition Car House as the reference for remapping and performance.

Generate more quote requests through the website.

Present services, projects and testimonials with credibility.

Build everything from the visual material the client already had.

03 — PROCESS

5 STEPS

A structure designed like a sales conversation.

01

References

Moodboard of workshops and automotive performance brands.

02

Architecture

Section order: offer, concept, strengths, services, projects and testimonials.

03

Art direction

Dark photos with smoke and light, the brand red and extended typography.

04

Layout

High-fidelity desktop interface at 1280 px.

05

Conversion

The same call to action, "Book your project", at the end of every section.

04 — VISUAL IDENTITY

Dark, smoke and red: art direction that feels like the racetrack.

05 — SOLUTION

DESKTOP, 1280 PX

A long page with rhythm: dark, light and red sections guide the reader to the quote request.

A clear offer up top

The main service appears in a single word, with the call to action right below.

Proof in every block

Workshop photos, projects and testimonials back up every promise.

A single call to action

The same button closes every section, so the next step is always clear.

06 — RESULTS

AFTER LAUNCH, 2025

+65%

increase in audience

with the new landing page

+30%

conversion into quote requests

2025

page live, with the brand repositioned

07 — LEARNINGS

Takeaway

A page that sells like a conversation. Ordering the content like a sale and repeating the same call to action at the end of each block helped turn visits into quote requests, even with little material from the client.
