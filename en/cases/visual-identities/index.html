CASE 05 · BRAND IDENTITY

Visual Identities

Five brands, five languages: a motorcycle workshop, a Veterinary Medicine graduation, a singer's album, a bar and my own studio.

ROLE

Visual identity and applications

CLIENT

05 real brands

YEAR

2025

TOOLS

Figma, Photoshop, Illustrator

01 — CONTEXT

LOGO · COLORS · TYPOGRAPHY · APPLICATIONS

Visual identity: a simple system that makes a brand recognizable before it is even read.

The work

Five identities in very different sectors: a motorcycle workshop, a veterinarian's graduation, a singer's album, a bar and my own studio, Vexa. Each project goes from the logo to its applications.

How I approach a brand

I start from what the brand needs to convey and translate it into a few elements: symbol, color and typography. They need to work together on any medium, from print to digital.

BRANDS

05 complete identities

SECTORS

Motorcycles, veterinary, music, food & drink and design

DELIVERABLES

Logo, colors, typography, guidelines and applications

02 — BSC METAL

MOTORCYCLE WORKSHOP · BRAND GUIDELINES

Speed and precision: a motorcycle in motion drawn over the brand name.

The brand

BSC Metal is a workshop specialized in motorcycles. The symbol is a fine-line motorcycle silhouette with speed trails, resting on the name set in geometric letters. Black and yellow bring the contrast of the workshop and the track.

SECTOR

Motorcycle workshop

DELIVERABLES

Logo, guidelines and applications

03 — LUIZ VET

VETERINARY MEDICINE GRADUATION

Made with the same care as someone who chose to care, to celebrate the end of one chapter and the start of a story.

The brand

Identity for Luiz Felipe Amaral Nakahara's graduation in Veterinary Medicine. The VET monogram has a leaf texture and the graduate's handwritten signature; a paw print appears as a detail. Green and a soft pink make the brand delicate without losing seriousness.

SECTOR

Graduation

04 — GIOVANNA CORREIA

ALBUM · BOOKLET AND CD

Blue and orange brushstrokes that bring color and movement to the album.

The project

Album identity for singer Giovanna Correia. The handwritten name, blue and orange paint strokes and portraits cut out by the brushstrokes form a light, handmade language that extends to the booklet and the CD.

SECTOR

Music

DELIVERABLES

Lettering, booklet and CD

FORMAT

Print

05 — BUTECO FOLKS

BAR & RESTAURANT · OPENING AND SOCIAL MEDIA

The most fun bar in Brazil: bright green, dark backgrounds and loud lettering.

The brand

Communication for Buteco Folks, a family-friendly bar with ice-cold draft beer and live music. The vintage-inspired badge, bright green on dark backgrounds and condensed uppercase typography gave the brand a lively voice in the opening posters and on social media.

SECTOR

Bar & restaurant

DELIVERABLES

Posters, posts and Instagram profile

FORMATS

Print and social media

06 — VEXA STUDIO

MY OWN STUDIO · CO-FOUNDED

Vexa Studio, my own studio: elegance, authenticity and performance for brands that want to convert.

The studio

Vexa Studio is the studio I co-founded with Rafael Omori in 2026 to create websites and brands. The identity had to be as direct as the offer: a strong name, a single green and a few pieces that work from the feed to the website.

2026
