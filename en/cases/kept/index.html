CASE 06 · DIGITAL PRODUCT · CONCEPT PROJECT

Kept

A system that helps small clinics lose fewer appointments: reminders with a confirmation link, a queue of what needs action and a waitlist that fills the slot on its own.

ROLE

Research, UX/UI and identity

PROJECT

Concept project

YEAR

2026

TOOLS

Figma, Claude

01 — CONTEXT

B2B SAAS · CLINICS WITH 2 TO 10 PROVIDERS

A no-show is a lost slot that another patient wanted.

The problem

In small clinics, the front desk confirms every appointment by hand on WhatsApp. When a patient doesn't reply, nobody knows if they're coming. When they don't show up, the slot sits empty and whoever was waiting for an opening never hears about it.

What the data says

A concept project with no client. So I started from published research, not made-up numbers.

NO-SHOWS

20% on average · Baptista et al., 2024

WITH REMINDERS

15% vs. 21% without · Robotham et al., 2016

PATIENT JOURNEY

67% of clinics never mapped it · Panorama 2026

02 — FLOW

3 LANES · PATIENT, KEPT AND FRONT DESK

From reminder to a full schedule, without the front desk chasing anyone.

How it works

The patient gets a reminder 48 h ahead, with a link to confirm, reschedule or cancel. With no reply, a 2nd reminder goes out 24 h ahead and the appointment joins the front desk queue. If someone cancels, the slot goes to the waitlist, one patient at a time, with a 2-hour window.

03 — WIREFRAMES

5 KEY SCREENS · GRAY BEFORE COLOR

Structure first, color later.

01

Status by icon and pattern

Not by color alone: the schedule stays readable for color-blind users and on paper.

02

Only what needs action gets queued

Confirmed appointments stay out. The front desk starts the day knowing exactly what to do.

03

One slot, one patient at a time

Offers follow the list order, with a deadline. Two patients never accept the same slot.

04 — IDENTITY

LOGO · COLORS · TYPE

A cross in one continuous stroke, with a dot at its heart: the appointment that was kept.

B · TYPE

SemiBold for the name and headings. Regular for interface text. Geometric and rounded: serious without being cold.

05 — INTERFACE

1440 PX · CLINIC FRONT DESK

Every status has an icon, a color and a pattern: the schedule reads in seconds.

A · DAY SCHEDULE

B · TO-DO QUEUE

C · WAITLIST

D · METRICS · SAMPLE DATA

06 — PATIENT

390 PX · NO LOGIN, NO APP

From the message link to a reply, in one tap.

Three states

Waiting, confirmed and expired link. Appointment details come before the options, so the patient recognizes what it's about. Confirming is the main action; rescheduling reads just as clearly, so people who can't make it don't simply vanish.

07 — HOW I'D MEASURE IT

CONCEPT PROJECT · NO MADE-UP RESULTS

No client means no results to show. This is what I'd track.

01

No-show rate

Target: below 10%. Compared with the month before Kept, by provider and by shift.

02

Confirmed by the day before

The earlier the reply, the more time to fill the slot again.

03

Recovered slots

Freed-up slots the waitlist managed to fill.

08 — TAKEAWAY

Cutting no-shows isn't about sending more messages: it's making clear what the front desk needs to do now.
