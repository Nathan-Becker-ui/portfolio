CASE 01 · CONCEPT PROJECT · WEBSITE & APP

B2F Travel Agency

Concept website and app for a fictional travel agency: destination search, packages, stays and a space to share travel moments.

ROLE

Brand, UX/UI and prototype

BRAND

B2F (fictional, created for this project)

YEAR

2024

TOOLS

Figma

01 — CONTEXT

WEBSITE & APP

From finding a destination to the next trip: a digital experience for B2F.

The brand

B2F (Born to Fly) is a fictional travel agency I created for this project. I defined its positioning, identity and digital experience: discovering destinations, packages, stays and sharing travel moments across website, app and smaller-screen versions.

The proposal

Create, from scratch, the brand and digital experience of a travel agency where travelers find destinations, compare packages and stays, and book the trip in one place. As a differentiator, a dedicated space, Momentos, where travelers share their trips and earn credits for the next one.

TYPE

Concept project: brand, website and app

AUDIENCE

Travelers looking for packages, stays and deals

GOAL

Bring together inspiration, booking and community

02 — CHALLENGE

4 GOALS

Bringing inspiration, booking and community into a single travel experience.

01

Make it easy to search by destination, dates and number of guests.

02

Present packages with flights and stays in an easy-to-compare way.

03

Highlight stays and deals for every season.

04

Create a space where travelers share moments and earn credits.

04 — VISUAL IDENTITY

SYMBOL, COLORS AND TYPOGRAPHY

Born to Fly: a B that is also a plane.

The name

B2F is short for Born to Fly: the 2 stands in for "to". The symbol turns this idea into form, joining the brand's initial with a plane drawn with as few strokes as possible.

Building the symbol

Two stacked triangles form the B. A third one, overlapping on the left, is the simplified plane. With equal stroke weights and rounded corners, the three make up the B2F symbol.

05 — INTERFACE

WEB PROTOTYPE · DESKTOP

From wireframe to interface: the structure stays, the brand sets the tone.

WIREFRAME

LOW FIDELITY

INTERFACE

HIGH FIDELITY

The full Home page

The page brings the whole journey together: search, deals, packages, stays, destinations and the invitation to Momentos.

Discover

Search by place, dates and guests opens the page, followed by deals and destinations.

Compare

Cards show destination, nights, price and installments side by side.

Share

Momentos invites travelers to share their trip and earn credits for the next one.

The Packages page

A showcase of packages: search opens the page, followed by this year's packages, best sellers and an invitation to use Momentos credits.

Filter

Destination search sits at the top, so travelers go straight to what they want.

Compare

Each card shows destination, nights, price, installments and what's included.

Convert

The credits block links the purchase to Momentos and encourages the next trip.

The Momentos page

The program where travelers share photos and videos of their trip and earn points to redeem for new trips.

Inspire

Travelers' photos open the page and show the spirit of the program.

Explain

Numbers and a step-by-step show how to earn and use points.

Engage

The sign-up form and the moments carousel close the page with social proof.

06 — LOG IN AND SIGN UP

WEB PROTOTYPE · DESKTOP

Log in and sign up in a few steps, with the same look as the website.

One field at a time

Log in asks for email or phone first, and only then for the password.

Visible shortcuts

Forgot password and create account sit right below the form.

Step-by-step sign up

Name, country, date of birth and email in a scrolling panel with a continue button.

08 — DELIVERABLES AND LEARNINGS

PROJECT SCOPE

Takeaway

The same language, at another scale. The smaller-screen versions rearrange navigation without losing B2F's identity. The next step is to validate the flows with users and refine the experience based on testing.
